Saturday 5 December 2026 · Vuda Community Hall

Viti Showcase and Skills Fair 2026 Show what you can make, build and do

A one-day event for students across the Western Division. Bring something you made, built or figured out and share it with teachers, families and local workers. Entry is free and anyone can join in.

  • Free entry
  • 8:30 am – 3:30 pm
  • All school years
See the schedule See the activities

Things you can do

Pick one or try all three. Students run them with help from teachers and local workers.

  • AutoCAD Design

    Show 2D and 3D drawings you made on the computer—like house plans, machine parts or room layouts. Explain how you measured and shaped your design while visitors take a look.

    Main hall · 9:30 am – 12:00 pm

  • Web Design

    Set up a laptop or tablet with a website or page you built from scratch. Walk visitors through your layout, colours, buttons and how it works on different screens.

    Verandah and courtyard · all day

  • Recipe Development

    Share a new dish, snack or drink you created using local or everyday ingredients. Explain what you changed while testing the recipe and let judges or visitors try a sample.

    Meeting room · 1:00 pm – 3:00 pm
